Getting started

  1. Open ThresholdIQ and follow the setup: units, your weekly schedule, seasons, events and race category.
  2. Connect Intervals.icu: in Intervals.icu, open Settings → Developer Settings and copy your Athlete ID and API key. Paste them into ThresholdIQ under You → Intervals.icu.
  3. Connect Hevy (optional): create an API key in Hevy's settings (a Hevy Pro feature) and paste it under You → Hevy.

Common questions

What are levels?
Each workout type (VO2 max, Threshold, SFR, Anaerobic, Endurance) has a level from 1 to 10. Finish every rep on target and the next one steps up; get most of it and it repeats; miss twice and it eases back.

How are rides graded?
After you ride, ThresholdIQ reads the intervals Intervals.icu detected in your ride and compares them with the planned workout. You'll see the result on Today.

How do I get a workout onto my bike computer?
Tap Send to Intervals.icu on Today or in a day of your Plan. Intervals.icu passes it on to Garmin, Wahoo, Zwift and other connected apps. Sending again updates the same workout.

Does it work with ROUVY?
Yes, through Intervals.icu. Connect Intervals.icu in ROUVY (Connected Apps and Devices on riders.rouvy.com). Today’s workout from your Intervals.icu calendar then shows up in ROUVY under Workouts, My Collections, Intervals.icu, and your ROUVY rides flow back to Intervals.icu, where ThresholdIQ picks them up.

Can I try it without an Intervals.icu account?
Yes. On the welcome screen, choose Explore with sample data to look around with a made-up rider. When you're ready, tap Use my own data on Today or You to clear the sample and set up with your own.

How do I move a workout to another day?
In Plan, tap the day and choose another day under Move this ride to swap them. You can also tell the coach, for example "I'm traveling Thursday" or "Only 45 minutes tomorrow". Put this week back as planned undoes your changes.

Can I change the order of things?
Yes. Tap ⇅ on Today or Progress to arrange the cards, drag a section's ≡ handle in a ride's details, and drag a food's ≡ handle onto another meal in Fuel.

Where do the Fuel numbers come from?
Your weight, your planned and finished rides, and any strength sessions. You can change how macros are set under Fuel settings. They're estimates to guide you, not a prescription.

Is my data private?
Yes. See the privacy policy: no accounts, no servers, no tracking.
