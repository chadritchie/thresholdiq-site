Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Short version: ThresholdIQ has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no tracking. Your training data stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of ThresholdIQ does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your personal data. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

  • Your training setup: units, weekly schedule, seasons, events, race category and goals.
  • Data you sync from Intervals.icu: athlete profile (FTP, heart rate thresholds, weight), daily wellness (Fitness, Fatigue, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, weight), activities, power curves and calendar workouts.
  • Data you sync from Hevy: strength workout titles, dates, durations and set counts.
  • Your levels and graded sessions, which ThresholdIQ works out on your device.
  • Your Intervals.icu and Hevy API keys, stored in the iOS Keychain.

This data stays on your device. It may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backups, according to your own backup settings.

Services you connect

When you connect Intervals.icu or Hevy, ThresholdIQ talks to them directly from your iPhone using the API key you enter. Nothing passes through our servers, because there aren't any. When you tap Send to Intervals.icu, the app writes that workout to your Intervals.icu calendar. Those services handle your data under their own privacy policies: Intervals.icu and Hevy.

Health and fitness data

Heart rate, HRV, sleep, weight and training data are used only to show your training and suggest workouts inside the app. They are never used for advertising, never sold and never shared.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to You and disconnect Intervals.icu and Hevy to remove your API keys and synced data. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored on your iPhone. Data in your Intervals.icu or Hevy accounts is managed in those services.

Children

ThresholdIQ is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
