ThresholdIQ brings progressive overload to cycling, the way a strength app does for lifting. Every workout type has a level from 1 to 10, and every ride you finish moves it.

Today

Know what to ride, and why

Fitness, Fatigue and Form at a glance, a readiness check from your sleep, HRV and resting heart rate, and a coach's note that explains the day in plain words.

Plan

A week that fits your life

Built from your schedule, season and races. Openers before race day, recovery after, and a lighter week every fourth.

  • Swap rides between days, or tell the coach "I'm traveling Thursday" or "only 45 minutes tomorrow".
  • Send any workout to your Intervals.icu calendar, and on to Garmin, Wahoo, Zwift or ROUVY.
Progress

Levels that move with you

Finish every rep on target and the next workout steps up. Get most of it and it repeats. Miss twice and it eases back. See your FTP inside your race category's band, your power curve and best efforts.

Fuel

Eat for the work you're doing

Daily calories and macros that follow your training, what to eat before, during and after a ride, and a quick log for your meals.
